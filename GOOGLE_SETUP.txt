Optional automatic course selection:
1. Open the Google Form editor.
2. Identify the entry ID of the "Select Course" question.
3. Put it in js/config.js: courseEntryId: "entry.XXXXXXXXX"
4. Test an ENROLL NOW button.

If courseEntryId is left empty, the same official Google Form opens normally.
